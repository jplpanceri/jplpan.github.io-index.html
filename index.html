About Emerald Escapes

Our Story

Emerald Escapes began with a simple idea: Ireland is best discovered slowly, through its people, places and stories. Founded in Galway in 2015 by two friends who spent years guiding visitors along the west coast, we saw that many travellers only ever saw the same few highlights. We set out to change that by creating trips that go beyond the postcard, from hidden coves on the Wild Atlantic Way to lively trad sessions in Dublin's oldest pubs.

Today we're a small, passionate team of local travel experts. We plan holidays, short breaks and unique experiences in all four provinces. Every trip is built on local knowledge, sustainable tourism and genuine Irish hospitality.

Who We Travel With

  • Couples and solo travellers looking for romantic getaways, city breaks or peaceful countryside retreats
  • Families who want safe, fun and stress-free adventures that both kids and adults will enjoy
  • Adventure seekers keen to hike, surf, kayak and explore Ireland's dramatic coastline
  • Culture lovers curious about Irish history, music, food and heritage
  • International visitors discovering Ireland for the first time, as well as Irish residents rediscovering their own country

Our Promise

Whether you have a weekend or a fortnight, we'll help you explore more and worry less. Your Irish adventure starts here.
